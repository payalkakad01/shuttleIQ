The Mixed doubles tournament of the 2026 BWF World Championships took place from 17 to 23 August 2026 at the Indira Gandhi Arena in New Delhi.[1][2]

Seeds

[edit]
See also: 2026 BWF World Championships qualification § Mixed doubles

The seeding list was based on the World Rankings of 31 July 2026.

  1. Feng Yanzhe / Huang Dongping (final)
  2. Jiang Zhenbang / Wei Yaxin (semi-finals)
  3. Mathias Christiansen / Alexandra Bøje (third round)
  4. Dechapol Puavaranukroh / Supissara Paewsampran (quarter-finals)
  5. Thom Gicquel / Delphine Delrue (champions)
  6. Guo Xinwa / Chen Fanghui (quarter-finals)
  7. Tang Chun Man / Tse Ying Suet (withdrew)
  8. Cheng Xing / Zhang Chi (quarter-finals)
  9. Ye Hong-wei / Nicole Gonzales Chan (third round)
  10. Goh Soon Huat / Shevon Jemie Lai (third round)
  11. Yang Po-hsuan / Hu Ling-fang (quarter-finals)
  12. Pakkapon Teeraratsakul / Sapsiree Taerattanachai (third round)
  13. Yuichi Shimogami / Sayaka Hobara (third round)
  14. Presley Smith / Jennie Gai (third round)
  15. Dhruv Kapila / Tanisha Crasto (third round)
  16. Yuta Watanabe / Maya Taguchi (third round)
