The Women's doubles tournament of the 2026 BWF World Championships took place from 17 to 23 August 2026 at the Indira Gandhi Arena in New Delhi.[1][2]

Seeds

[edit]
See also: 2026 BWF World Championships qualification § Women's doubles

The seeding list was based on the World Rankings of 31 July 2026.

  1. Liu Shengshu / Tan Ning (final)
  2. Yuki Fukushima / Mayu Matsumoto (third round)
  3. Baek Ha-na / Lee So-hee (champions)
  4. Jia Yifan / Zhang Shuxian (quarter-finals)
  5. Pearly Tan / Thinaah Muralitharan (third round)
  6. Kim Hye-jeong / Kong Hee-yong (withdrew)
  7. Rin Iwanaga / Kie Nakanishi (third round)
  8. Li Yijing / Luo Xumin (semi-finals)
  9. Gabriela Stoeva / Stefani Stoeva (third round)
  10. Hsieh Pei-shan / Hung En-tzu (third round)
  11. Hsu Yin-hui / Lin Jhih-yun (third round)
  12. Rachel Allessya Rose / Febi Setianingrum (quarter-finals)
  13. Febriana Dwipuji Kusuma / Meilysa Trias Puspita Sari (quarter-finals)
  14. Rui Hirokami / Sayaka Hobara (third round)
  15. Hsu Ya-ching / Sung Yu-hsuan (third round)
  16. Lauren Lam / Allison Lee (second round)
