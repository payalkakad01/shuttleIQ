The Men's doubles tournament of the 2026 BWF World Championships is currently taking place from 17 to 23 August 2026 at the Indira Gandhi Arena in New Delhi.[1][2]


Seeds

[edit]
See also: 2026 BWF World Championships qualification § Men's doubles

The seeding list was based on the World Rankings of 31 July 2026.

  1. Kim Won-ho / Seo Seung-jae (semi-finals)
  2. Fajar Alfian / Muhammad Shohibul Fikri (quarter-finals)
  3. Liang Weikeng / Wang Chang (champions)
  4. Aaron Chia / Soh Wooi Yik (final)
  5. Satwiksairaj Rankireddy / Chirag Shetty (quarter-finals)
  6. Goh Sze Fei / Nur Izzuddin (quarter-finals)
