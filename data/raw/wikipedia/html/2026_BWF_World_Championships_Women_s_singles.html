The Women's singles tournament of the 2026 BWF World Championships took place from 17 to 23 August 2026 at the Indira Gandhi Arena in New Delhi.[1][2]

Seeds

[edit]
See also: 2026 BWF World Championships qualification § Women's singles

The seeding list was based on the World Rankings of 31 July 2026.

  1. An Se-young (champion)
  2. Akane Yamaguchi (final)
  3. Wang Zhiyi (semi-finals)
  4. Chen Yufei (third round)
  5. Han Yue (quarter-finals)
  6. Putri Kusuma Wardani (third round)
  7. Ratchanok Intanon (second round)
  8. Tomoka Miyazaki (third round)
  9. P. V. Sindhu (third round)
  10. Pornpawee Chochuwong (semi-finals)

  12. Kim Ga-eun (third round)
  13. Michelle Li (quarter-finals)
  14. Sim Yu-jin (third round)
  15. Line Christophersen (quarter-finals)
  16. Mia Blichfeldt (third round)
